### Wind speed — 20 m/s

**Temperature:** Almost nothing.

**Velocity z:** Clear alternating upward/downward motions near the surface.

**Why:** Higher wind speed increases shear and mechanically generated turbulence.

*higher velocity wind creates more disturbance near the ground*

---

### Surface roughness — `z0 = 0.75 m`

**Temperature:** Almost nothing.

**Velocity z:** Pattern changes and becomes less intense/more diffuse near the surface.

**Why:** Higher roughness increases surface drag and changes the near-surface shear.

*more roughness in the terrain slows air down*
---

### ABL/inversion height — 250 m

**Temperature:** Clear change; the temperature transition moves downward.

**Velocity z:** Almost nothing.

**Why:** Changing the inversion height directly changes the temperature structure, but has little immediate effect on the near-surface vertical velocity in this short run.
