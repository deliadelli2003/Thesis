## Reynolds-stress averaging results

The following figures show the six components stored in the Kynema-SGF output as `velocity_reynolds_stress_averaging0` to `velocity_reynolds_stress_averaging5`.

For each component, two planes are presented:

- an \(x\)-\(z\) vertical plane at \(y=1280\ \mathrm{m}\);
- an \(x\)-\(y\) horizontal plane at \(z=100\ \mathrm{m}\).

The Reynolds stresses have units of \(\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-2}}\) and describe the intensity and correlation of the turbulent velocity fluctuations.
